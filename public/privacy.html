Twivvy privacy policy

Effective 17 September 2026. Publisher: Dzmitry Sharko.

The Twivvy iOS app has no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking, accounts, or in-app purchases.

Information used to play

Your chosen nickname and preferences are stored on your device. When you play online, Twivvy sends your nickname, a randomly generated room or matchmaking credential, and connection information to its signaling service, hosted on Google Cloud. Your nickname is visible to your opponent. WebRTC exchanges moves directly between players and can reveal each player's IP address to the other player. Google STUN servers help establish this connection.

The signaling service stores room and matchmaking information to connect players and recover interrupted games. It does not process game moves. Waiting rooms and games with an absent player expire after 30 minutes; active rooms remain available. Service infrastructure may retain request logs, including IP addresses, for operation and security. Twivvy does not use these records for advertising or profiling.

The browser game is delivered through Cloudflare, which may process request metadata and browser performance measurements to operate and monitor the website. These support and privacy pages do not load analytics scripts.

Local storage and deletion

The iOS app stores online recovery credentials, match state and hidden commitments in the Keychain on this device. Choosing to leave the online game clears those recovery records and requests removal of the room. Preferences use local iOS storage. The web version uses browser storage for preferences and recovery. Training works offline, although an optional presence check may contact the signaling service to show whether a human player is waiting.

Device permissions

Twivvy does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. The Share button opens the system share sheet. Sound and haptic feedback can be turned off in Settings.

Your choices and contact

You can play offline, use a nickname that does not identify you, leave an online room, and clear browser storage. For support, privacy questions or deletion requests, contact privacy@e-notix.com. Do not include secret room credentials in email.
