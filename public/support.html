Twivvy support

A little twist. A big rivalry.

For help with Twivvy or a privacy request, email privacy@e-notix.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and a description of the problem. Do not send room recovery credentials.

How to play

Rotate one of nine platforms each round to send balls into your receiver. Both players choose secretly. Their rotations are revealed together after 30 seconds. A platform used this round is unavailable next round. Seven points wins early; otherwise the higher score after 30 rounds wins.

Joining a friend

Choose “Play with a friend” and share the room link. On iPhone or iPad, choose “Have an invite?” and paste the room code or link. Browser players can open the same link directly. Both players must stay online.

Connection problems

Return to the app and allow it to reconnect. You can resume a saved game from the main menu. Waiting rooms and matches with an absent player expire after 30 minutes. A restrictive network may block direct WebRTC connections; try Wi-Fi or cellular data. Training works offline.

Sound

Enable Sound in Settings and check your device’s Silent Mode and volume. Twivvy respects Silent Mode and mixes with your music.
